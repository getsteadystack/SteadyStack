Unsaid — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Version: 1.0

Developer: Alex

Unsaid is a conversation-card app for iPhone, iPad, and Android: 31 free decks with 310 questions written by hand, plus 10 premium decks, depth filters, and saved cards. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it — and, mostly, what doesn't happen to it.

The short version

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up screen, no name, no email address.
  • Your cards stay on your phone. Saved cards, settings, and your purchase unlock are stored on your device, not in a server we own.
  • We never see your payment details. Premium decks are bought through the App Store or Google Play.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling data. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

If a detail below ever seems to contradict the short version, the detail wins. If anything is unclear, email madajoe6969@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Unsaid is developed and published by Alex. For anything in this policy, contact us at madajoe6969@gmail.com. Where privacy laws give you rights (the GDPR in the EEA and UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, and similar laws elsewhere), we are the party responsible for the limited processing described here.

This policy covers the Unsaid app itself. The App Store and Google Play are separate services with their own privacy policies.

2. What we don't collect

Because Unsaid has no accounts, there is nothing to sign up with and no profile to build. We do not collect or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • passwords, passkeys, or any other login credentials — Unsaid never asks for any;
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or files;
  • advertising identifiers, or any ID that tracks you across other apps or websites;
  • anything you say during a conversation. The app is a deck of cards to read aloud — it does not listen, record, or transmit what you talk about.

3. What's stored on your device

Unsaid keeps a few things locally so it works offline and picks up where you left off:

  • Saved cards — the questions you bookmark.
  • Settings — depth filter, theme, and similar preferences.
  • Purchase unlock — a note that you own a premium deck, so it opens without an internet connection.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We have no server that receives it and no way to read it. There is no sync service, so your saved cards live on the device where you saved them. Two things worth knowing:

  • Device backups. If your phone is set to back itself up (iCloud or Google backup), your device backup may include app data. That backup is governed by your platform provider's terms, not this policy.
  • Deleting it. Removing a saved card removes it immediately. Uninstalling Unsaid deletes everything it stored on your device.

4. Premium decks and payments

The premium decks are sold as in-app purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

  • Apple or Google processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number, or billing address.
  • We receive a purchase confirmation — essentially "this store account bought this deck" — which the app uses to unlock what you bought and to restore purchases when you reinstall or switch devices.
  • Refunds, payment methods, and purchase history are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

5. App updates and content delivery

To keep Unsaid current, the app may check for and download updates or deck content over the internet, delivered through our app-update service (Expo Application Services).

Like any request on the internet, those requests carry your IP address and basic technical details — platform, app version, and which file was requested. That's what any download involves. It is used only to deliver the content and keep the service healthy: it isn't used to identify you, and it is never combined with anything else, because we hold nothing else.

6. Analytics, ads, and tracking

Unsaid ships with no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics. We don't measure how you move through the app; we can't see which decks you open, and nothing you do in the app is reported anywhere. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, because we don't have any.

One thing that happens outside the app: your phone's operating system may ask whether to share crash and diagnostic reports with app developers. If you opt in and the app crashes, Apple or Google may pass us an aggregate crash report (what crashed, on which OS version). Those reports contain no personal information, and we can't connect them to you.

7. Children's privacy

Unsaid is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent where your country sets one). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information some other way — for example by email — contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your privacy rights

If you live somewhere with privacy laws — the EEA, the UK, California, Brazil, Canada, and others — you may have the right to access, correct, delete, move, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to processing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice:

  • There is no account and no profile to look up. What Unsaid knows about your use lives on your device and is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in California's privacy law, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.
  • Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. To manage or delete purchase records, use the store's account settings.
  • Questions or requests: email madajoe6969@gmail.com. We'll respond within the time the law allows, though we may have to ask a clarifying question — we genuinely have no files to search.

9. Security and retention

Your saved cards and settings get the protection your device already provides — passcode, biometrics, full-disk encryption. Because we don't operate a user database, there is no copy of your information on our side to leak, lose, or retain. Locally stored data lives until you delete it; uninstalling removes all of it. Purchase records held by Apple or Google follow their retention policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If Unsaid changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if a future version adds optional analytics or accounts — we will update this document, change the Effective date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes. Material changes will get an in-app notice. Previous versions are available on request. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions, concerns, or privacy requests: madajoe6969@gmail.com. We're a small team, and we read everything.
