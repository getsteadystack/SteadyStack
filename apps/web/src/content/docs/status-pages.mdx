SteadyStack status pages give your customers transparent, real-time insight into your system availability while keeping your on-call team focused on fixes rather than customer support replies.

---

## Features

- **Custom Domains with Free SSL**: Bind any domain (e.g., `status.yourcompany.com`) with automated Cloudflare edge certificate provisioning.
- **Access Control**: Choose between public status pages or password-protected private pages for enterprise clients.
- **SLA & Uptime Timelines**: Choose 30, 60, or 90-day interactive lookback bars showing incident duration and response times.
- **Component Grouping**: Group monitors by infrastructure tier (e.g., "Core APIs", "Authentication", "Payment Gateway").
- **Subscriber Notifications**: Allow users to subscribe to incident updates via email and webhook.
