Welcome to **SteadyStack for Agencies**. Whether you manage 10 client websites or 500+ client production applications across WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, and custom APIs, SteadyStack is built to turn uptime monitoring into a high-margin client retainer service.

Traditional monitoring tools charge prohibitive per-monitor fees and spam your engineering team with 3:00 AM localized network blips. SteadyStack provides:

1. **Zero False Alarms**: Multi-region edge quorum verification stops phantom alerts.
2. **Client Scoping & Tagging**: Organize monitors by client name, SLA tier, and billing cycle.
3. **Automated Monthly Executive PDFs**: Prove your retainer's value with auto-generated SLA reports delivered directly to your clients.
4. **Branded White-Label Portals**: Host client status pages on your own agency domain (e.g., `status.youragency.com`) with your custom logo, typography, and theme.

## 1. Setting Up Your Agency Workspace

When you create your account, configure your agency workspace settings:

1. Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Organization**.
2. Set your **Agency Brand Name** and upload your **Primary Logo**.
3. Choose your default notification routing channels (e.g., Agency Slack `#client-alerts` channel).
4. Invite your agency team members with role-based access control (Admin, Developer, Viewer).

## 2. Organizing Client Monitors with Tagging

Use structured namespaces for tags to keep hundreds of client websites organized:

- `client:<client-name>` (e.g. `client:acme-corp`, `client:nike-retail`)
- `tier:<sla-level>` (e.g. `tier:gold`, `tier:silver`, `tier:bronze`)
- `stack:<tech>` (e.g. `stack:wordpress`, `stack:shopify`, `stack:nextjs`)

```bash
# Example creating a tagged client monitor via SteadyStack CLI
steadystack monitor create \
  --name "Acme Corp E-Commerce Store" \
  --url "https://store.acmecorp.com" \
  --interval 60 \
  --tags "client:acme-corp,tier:gold,stack:shopify"
```

## 3. Creating a Client-Specific White-Label Status Page

Give your client a real-time branded window into their infrastructure availability:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Status Pages → Create New Status Page**.
2. Assign a custom slug or bind your custom subdomain (e.g. `acme.agency-status.com`).
3. Select which monitors belong to this client using the `client:acme-corp` tag filter.
4. Choose an editorial theme (such as **Sentry Dark**, **Loops Light**, or your custom agency hex colors).
5. Set the access mode:
   - **Public**: Accessible to all client team members.
   - **Password Protected**: Guarded with a client-specific passcode.
   - **Restricted Token**: Generated view token with revocable access.

## 5. Agency Best Practices

> [!TIP]
> **Combine Homepage & Cache-Busting Health Checks**
> Don't just ping the homepage. For CMS sites like WordPress, monitor `https://client.com/` for CDN/DNS availability and `https://client.com/wp-json/wp/v2/types` or an uncached API endpoint to catch PHP-FPM and database connection dropouts.

- **Set 60-Second Quorum Intervals**: Instant incident detection without false alarm risk.
- **Configure Separate Alert Rules**: Route critical production outages to PagerDuty / SMS, and minor latency degradations to internal agency Slack channels.
- **Track SSL Expiration**: Set 30-day SSL expiry alerts so your agency renews certificates before client browsers display security warnings.
