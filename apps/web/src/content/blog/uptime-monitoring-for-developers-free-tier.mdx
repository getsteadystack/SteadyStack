## The 4 Hallmarks of Developer-First Uptime Monitoring

When developers evaluate synthetic monitoring tools for their microservices, APIs, and websites, four non-negotiable requirements stand out:

### 1. Fast Check Intervals (Not 5-Minute Polling)

A 5-minute check interval is virtually useless for modern production apps. If your Redis cache crashes or a deployment fails at 12:01, a 5-minute poller won't discover the issue until 12:05.

Developers need:

- **60-Second Fast Checks:** Standard checks running every 1 to 3 minutes, with 60-second intervals on your most critical endpoints.
- **Immediate Detection:** Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) reduced from minutes to seconds.
