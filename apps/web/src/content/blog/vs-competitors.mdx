## The Three Core SteadyStack Differentiators

### 1. High-Frequency Checks & Rapid Detection

While legacy competitors restrict free accounts to 5-minute sampling (300 seconds), SteadyStack offers 1-minute checks for your first 10 critical monitors and 3-minute checks across remaining targets (with 30-second rapid polling on paid plans). That means faster incident detection and zero blind spots for transient micro-outages.

### 2. Edge Quorum Consensus Voting

Instead of relying on a single probe node, SteadyStack verifies failures across geographically pinned Cloudflare edge Durable Objects in parallel. An alert is only dispatched when multi-region quorum confirms the failure, eliminating false alarm fatigue.

### 3. Flat, Developer-Friendly Pricing

SteadyStack does not charge per seat or penalize growing engineering teams. Paid plans scale on flat monitor capacity with generous limits.
