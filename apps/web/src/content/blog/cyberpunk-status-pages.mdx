Status pages have historically been an afterthought in DevOps tooling: a white box with green and red dots, maybe an unstyled timeline if you're lucky. Functional, sure — but they look like software built in 2010.

We think your status page deserves better. It is often the first place your customers go when something goes wrong. It should be clear, calming, and visually exceptional.

---

## Five Themes, One Status Page

Every SteadyStack status page comes with five out-of-the-box visual themes:

| Theme Name    | Style Aesthetic               | Best Used For                   |
| :------------ | :---------------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| **Dark**      | Sleek zinc / emerald contrast | Modern SaaS and cloud developer |
| **Light**     | Crisp minimal paper           | Enterprise & corporate portals  |
| **Cyberpunk** | High-contrast neon aesthetics | Gaming, crypto, & devtools      |
| **Matrix**    | Retro terminal green phosphor | CLI & open-source projects      |
| **Blade**     | Deep obsidian & icy cyan      | Modern AI & high-tech startups  |

Users can toggle between themes with a single click — no page reloads or configuration changes required.

---

## Designed for Incident Clarity

When an outage happens, clarity is paramount. Our status pages surface the most important information first:

- **Overall Health Indicator:** Clear, color-coded operational state at the top.
- **Service Component Breakdown:** Granular visibility into API, Web, DB, and CDN subsystems.
- **Interactive Latency Graphs:** Historical 24-hour and 30-day response time curves.
- **Incident Timeline:** Real-time chronological status updates with formatted markdown notes.

---

## Custom Domains & Instant SSL

Your status page should live at `status.yourdomain.com`, not on a third-party vendor subdomain. SteadyStack includes automatic SSL certificate generation and custom domain routing on our free plan.

Bring your own logo, custom colors, and favicon to make your status page feel like an integrated part of your product.
